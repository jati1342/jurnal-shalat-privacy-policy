Privacy Policy for Jurnal Shalat Madrasah

Last updated: September 2026

Jurnal Shalat Madrasah ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to minimize the data we collect from our users to ensure a safe and private user experience:

  • Personal Information: We do not require users to create an account or submit personal information (such as your full name, email address, or phone number) to use the core features of this app.
  • Local Data Storage: Data entered into the app (such as prayer tracking, notes, or madrasah schedules) is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to external servers operated by us.
  • Device Permissions:
    • Notifications: Used solely to send local reminders for prayer times or app notifications.
    • Location (Optional): If enabled, location access is used locally on your device strictly to calculate accurate prayer times based on your current geographical coordinates.

2. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party tools or SDKs (such as Google Play Services or analytics tools) to maintain app stability and performance. These third parties may collect non-personally identifiable information, such as device model, OS version, and crash logs.

3. Data Security

Because your tracking data remains on your local device, you retain direct control over your information. You can clear your app data or uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored information.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our app is designed to be suitable for general audiences, including students. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL and will take effect immediately upon posting.
